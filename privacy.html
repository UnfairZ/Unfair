Privacy

What we store

When you sign in, we store your Discord account ID, username, display name, and the dates you first and last signed in. We use this information to recognize your account and show your profile.

How sign-in works

Discord handles sign-in on its own site. We do not receive your password or email address, and we do not save Discord access or refresh tokens. Our site uses a short-lived cookie to finish sign-in and a separate cookie to keep you signed in for up to seven days. Signing out ends that website session.

Account requests

Your profile stays on file so you can sign in again. Contact staff through the Unfair Advantage Discord server to ask about your data or request deletion. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The account service is hosted on Cloudflare.
